Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

MinutesMap is built to be lightweight and privacy-focused. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information like names, email addresses, or payment details.

Short version: We collect minimal analytics and store a temporary connection ID during live games. Preferences are stored locally in your browser.

Information We Collect

  • Analytics: We use analytics tools to understand site usage and improve the product.
  • Local preferences: We store your play-by-play stat display preferences in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers your settings.
  • Live connection state: While you are connected to a live game, we store a non-identifying connection ID to manage real-time updates. This is removed when you disconnect.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, or payment information. We do not use advertising pixels or sell personal data.

Client-Side Storage

We use localStorage for your preferences and analytics state.

Service Providers

We use trusted hosting and delivery providers to run the site and serve content quickly. These providers may process basic request data (such as IP address and user agent) as part of normal service operation.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date.
