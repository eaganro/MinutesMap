Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

MinutesMap does not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for personal information like names, email addresses, or payment details.

Short version: We use PostHog to measure how the site is used, which includes recordings of on-page sessions. We store a temporary connection ID during live games. Your display preferences are stored locally in your browser.

Information We Collect

  • Usage analytics: We use PostHog to record page views, the games and dates you view, which features you use, clicks, and how long a page stays open. Each browser is assigned a random identifier so visits can be grouped together. PostHog also receives your IP address and browser details, and may use them to estimate an approximate location (such as country or city).
  • Session recordings: PostHog records how you interact with the page (mouse movement, clicks, scrolling, and what is shown on screen) so we can find bugs and improve the layout. Text you type into form fields is masked.
  • Error reports: If something breaks, we send the error details and the page you were on to PostHog so we can fix it.
  • Local preferences: We store your display settings (such as dark mode and which stats are shown) in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers them.
  • Live connection state: While you are following a live game, we store a non-identifying connection ID to send you real-time updates. It is deleted when you disconnect, and automatically expires within 12 hours in any case.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, or payment information. We do not use advertising pixels, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.

Cookies and Client-Side Storage

We do not set cookies. We use localStorage for your preferences and for PostHog's random identifier and session state. You can clear this at any time from your browser settings.

Service Providers

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the site, serves content, and runs live updates. Like any web host, it processes basic request data such as IP address and user agent.
  • PostHog (United States) processes the analytics, session recordings, and error reports described above. Analytics requests are routed through our own domain before being forwarded to PostHog.

Opting Out

The site works normally if you block analytics requests. If you would like recordings or analytics associated with your browser deleted, email us and we will do our best to help.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date.
